🈊 Apo Island Tips

🏭 Reef-safe only
Chemical sunscreens harm coral. Rangers may turn you away — use mineral SPF.
💸 Bring cash pesos
No ATMs on the island. You'll need cash for the environmental fee and food.
⏱ Go early
Morning crossings have calmer seas. Afternoon winds can make the ride choppy.
